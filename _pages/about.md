Hi, I'm **voidvein**. My research focuses on deep learning for computer vision, with a particular interest in **human pose estimation**.

I am interested in understanding people in images and videos through models of human structure and motion. I approach research as a gradual process of asking questions, testing ideas, and learning from the results.

## Research Interests

**Human pose estimation** is the main focus of my current work. I am interested in how visual models represent human pose, and in understanding their performance and limitations in challenging scenes.

More recently, I have become interested in **world models**: how models learn the dynamics of an environment and predict future states from observations. I am still learning about this area, particularly its potential connections to understanding human motion.

## About This Site

This site is a place for research notes, reflections on what I read, and work in progress. My aim is to develop a clearer understanding of the problems I study and to test ideas carefully through practice.

I welcome discussions about computer vision, human pose estimation, and world models. You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/voidvein).
