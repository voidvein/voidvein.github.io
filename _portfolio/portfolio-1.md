QuantFarm Visual Studio explores how AI-assisted visual creation can be organized around the branding needs of agricultural products. The interface brings together entry points for brand identity, packaging, 3D modeling, livestream settings, and retail spaces.

[Visit the project](http://114.116.246.186/)

## Project Overview

An agricultural product needs more than a single image to communicate its identity. Packaging, product displays, and the spaces in which it is presented all contribute to the same story. QuantFarm frames these as related creative tasks within one visual workspace.

The homepage combines a persistent navigation panel, creative categories, and a gallery of examples. Tea, fruit, grains, and honey provide the context for exploring a consistent visual language across different formats.

## Visual Directions

The examples displayed on the site span three closely related areas:

- **Packaging and identity:** gift boxes, containers, and surface graphics for agricultural products.
- **Product form:** packaging structures and 3D presentation concepts.
- **Spatial presentation:** livestream settings and retail interiors that extend the product's visual identity.

## Perspective

The project presents an application-oriented view of visual AI: placing creative tools within the context of a product and its brand, rather than treating each image as an isolated result. A central question is how to make the relationship between these tasks clear and approachable in the interface.
